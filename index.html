Economics, Charles University. Tools that find the problems in a research draft before a referee, a panel, or an examiner does.

  • Paper An adversarial audit: four models read it in different roles, then cross-examine each other.
  • ERC draft Unofficial pre-review of a Starting or Consolidator proposal, before you submit.
  • GA UK application Unofficial check of a Charles University grant application, in Czech or English.
  • Thesis What your supervisor and opponent are likely to raise, before the seminar.

Each page hands you a prompt to paste into a chatbot. This site stores nothing; what you paste goes to the provider you choose.

Deeper, on GitHub

  • paper-workshop CRUCIBLE: referees from rival traditions argue a paper to the sentence, then rebuild it. Needs Claude Code.
  • mad-research Claude and Codex audit a paper against each other, and a fresh model judges what survived. Needs Claude Code and Codex.
  • meta-analysis.cz Our meta-analyses in full: 71 papers, 46 datasets with 58,405 estimates, and the code. CC BY 4.0.
